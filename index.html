Workflow

How it works

1 · Online store
The customer orders from the NBA Store website.
1
Choose team
Pick one of the 30 teams and a team color or black card.
2
Personalize
Engrave a name or choose a player signature.
3
Pay
Checkout with a simulated payment.
4
Get the QR code
The order is saved and a QR code with the order number is shown.
QR code carries the order number from the store to the kiosk
2 · Kiosk
On site, next to the robot.
5
Scan
The customer scans the QR code or types the order number.
6
Check or edit
The order loads with team, card and engraving. The name or the signature can be corrected.
7
Engrave
After confirmation the robot engraves the card, front and back.
8
Take the card
The order is marked as engraved.
Order status paid, then engraved, is recorded for every order
3 · Sales dashboard
For the operator.
9
Track sales
Orders, revenue, best days and hours, and sales by team and by player signature.
10
Royalties
Royalties per team and per player are calculated on engraved cards and exported to CSV or XLSX.
